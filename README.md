# Singapore–China Due Diligence (Mock Case Study)

## Investigative Due Diligence & Business Intelligence Portfolio

This repository contains a fictional cross-border investigative due diligence project built using Microsoft SQL Server. It simulates the type of corporate intelligence work undertaken before private equity investments, acquisitions, strategic partnerships, or cross-border transactions.

**Disclaimer:** All companies, individuals, ownership structures, and transactions in this repository are fictional and created solely for portfolio and educational purposes.

## Client scenario

A private equity client is considering an investment into a Singapore-based infrastructure company with significant operations in China and Southeast Asia.

The investigation seeks to identify material non-financial risks, including:

* Political exposure (PEPs and government connections)
* Government-linked commercial relationships
* Offshore ownership and Ultimate Beneficial Ownership (UBO) risk
* Cross-border governance and transparency concerns

## Key findings

The investigation identified:

* High-risk political connections within senior management
* Government-linked commercial contracts requiring enhanced review
* 62% offshore ownership through a British Virgin Islands entity
* Overall investment risk rating: **CRITICAL**

## Risk methodology

The executive dashboard applies a weighted risk model across three investigative categories:

* Political & management exposure
* Government-linked contracts
* Offshore ownership / UBO transparency

The final output produces an executive risk rating together with a recommended risk response consistent with enhanced due diligence practice.
